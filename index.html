1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to Repeatify Pro ("we", "our", or "us"), developed and published by SumDev Studio. We recognize that your privacy is of the utmost importance. This Privacy Policy details how Repeatify Pro collects, handles, stores, and protects your information when you download, install, and use our mobile application (Package ID: com.repeatify.sumdev.app).

✨
Our Core Privacy Promise: We believe your generated text, messages, and stylistic creations belong strictly to you. All text repetition algorithms, ASCII emoji arts, font transformations, and number decorations run 100% locally on your device. We never read, upload, track, or save your text content on any cloud server.

By accessing or using Repeatify Pro, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

🔒

2. Information We NEVER Collect

Unlike many utilities that send user inputs to remote APIs, Repeatify Pro is engineered from the ground up for strict on-device data isolation. We do NOT collect or access:

✍️ Generated Text
Whatever you type or repeat stays in device RAM and is never stored on external servers.
📍 GPS Location
We never ask for or track precise or approximate geographical location.
📸 Camera / Mic
No audio recordings, camera capture, or biometric sensors are ever utilized.
📇 Contacts & Phone
WhatsApp Direct opens chat via standard intent without reading your contact book.
📊

3. Data We Collect & How It Is Used

To provide cloud synchronization for purchased coins, user profiles, and app reliability, we collect limited categories of data:

  • Account & Authentication Information: When you voluntarily register or sign in, we process your Email Address and account creation timestamps via Google Firebase Authentication. This ensures your coin balance and VIP status can be safely restored if you switch or reinstall devices.
  • Coin Vault & Purchase Records: When you purchase virtual coins through Google Play In-App Billing, a cryptographic receipt and order identifier is verified and synced to Google Cloud Firestore under your account. We never receive or store your credit card, debit card, or bank account numbers.
  • Anonymous Crash & Performance Diagnostics: We utilize Firebase Crashlytics to identify app freezes, memory allocation bottlenecks (such as during massive 50,000x repeat computations), and unexpected crashes. This includes device brand, Android OS version, and crash stack traces. No personally identifiable information is linked to crash diagnostics.
🌐

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work exclusively with certified industry leaders to deliver backend and payment infrastructure:

  • Google Play In-App Billing: Handles all in-app transactions, payment processing, tax calculation, and purchase security. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Firebase: Provides user authentication (Firebase Auth), database persistence (Cloud Firestore), and error telemetry (Firebase Crashlytics). (Firebase Privacy Overview)
🔐

5. Data Security & Storage

All network transmissions between Repeatify Pro and Firebase servers are strictly protected using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) encryption. Your password is never stored in plain text and is salted and hashed by Google's secure authentication infrastructure.

Local settings, themes, and sound preferences are stored locally on your device in secure sandbox storage (Android SharedPreferences) and remain inaccessible to other applications.

👶

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Repeatify Pro is intended for general audiences interested in creative typography and messaging fun. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us at sumdev.studio.official@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete such records from our servers.

🗑️

7. User Rights & Account Deletion (In-App & Online)

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA), you have complete ownership of your data. You can delete your account, authentication records, and cloud coin vault permanently at any time using either our built-in instant self-service option or via email.

⚡
Method 1: Instant In-App Account Deletion (Self-Service)

You can permanently delete your account directly from within Repeatify Pro in real time:

  1. Open the Repeatify Pro app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your User Avatar on the top bar of the Home Screen to open your Profile (or navigate to Settings > Delete Account & Data).
  3. Tap "Delete Account Permanently".
  4. To prevent accidental deletion, type CONFIRM in capital letters into the confirmation box.
  5. Tap the "DELETE PERMANENTLY" button.

Result: Your Firebase Authentication credentials, active session tokens, local preferences, and synced coin balances are completely and irreversibly erased immediately.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request (Alternative)

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, send an email from your registered address to request permanent manual deletion of your records within 48 hours.
